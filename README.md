# Lead Intelligence Tool

A modern lead generation and intelligence platform built to help sales teams discover, evaluate, prioritize, save, and engage with potential business leads.

This project was developed as part of the Caprae Capital AI-readiness pre-screening challenge, with a focus on practical lead-generation workflows and AI-powered sales assistance.

---
##  Live link 
https://lead-intelligence-tool-w4tz.vercel.app/

##  Project Overview

The Lead Intelligence Tool improves the traditional lead-generation workflow by combining:

- Real company discovery
- Lead scoring
- Lead prioritization
- Search and filtering
- Lead management
- CSV export
- Saved leads
- AI-powered personalized outreach

The main goal is to help sales teams spend less time manually evaluating leads and more time engaging with high-potential prospects.

---

##  Key Features

### 1. Lead Dashboard

Provides an overview of the lead database:

- Total leads
- Hot leads
- Warm leads
- Average lead score
- Cold leads
- Top industry
- Top location
- High-priority lead percentage
- Lead priority analytics
- Top-scoring leads

---

### 2. Smart Lead Scoring

Each lead receives a score from **0–100** based on four factors:

| Factor | Maximum Points |
|---|---:|
| Employee Size | 25 |
| Revenue | 25 |
| Contact Role | 25 |
| Industry Fit | 25 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Leads are automatically categorized:

```text
80–100 → HOT
60–79  → WARM
0–59   → COLD
